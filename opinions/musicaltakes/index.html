    Thomas Reid:

  • 2017 - Fall

    • will probably always be one of my favorite albums (possibly ever).

    There is something profoundly moving in this album, and I haven’t quite found out what yet. A poetic masterpiece that feels etched and connected to the music itself. It has stuck around me ever since I gave it a first listen, and I’d argue that it’s grown me into the person I am currently. If you give it a listen to on a walk—or maybe a particularly grey day, you can feel the love and passion Thomas has for this album. It has an incredibly raw, intimate feel. Brings me to a state of accepting overwhelm each time. It has influenced me as a musician, and as a person; an album I put on when I’ve lost myself, as a reminder to what I’ve lost, and what I’ve gained over the years since I listened to it. Maybe you’ll feel the same, maybe you won’t. It is still worth a listen.
